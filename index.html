1. Overview

Welcome to Auto Touch. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android application.

2. AccessibilityService API Usage Disclosure

This section explains Auto Touch's use of Android's AccessibilityService API:

• Purpose of Accessibility Service: Auto Touch uses the AccessibilityService API strictly to dispatch simulated tap and swipe gestures at specific screen coordinates configured by you.

• Accessibility data handling: The Accessibility Service does not read screen text, record keystrokes, access passwords or contacts, or collect location. It only dispatches the tap and swipe gestures you configure. This statement applies to the Accessibility Service; advertising SDK data handling is described below.

• Local Storage Only: All your custom configuration settings and swipe/tap target information are stored exclusively within your device's local storage (AutoClickStore) and are never sent to external servers.

• Third-Party Services: Auto Touch integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

3. Overlay Permission (Display Over Other Apps)

Auto Touch requests the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display Over Other Apps") permission solely to display the floating action controller and target pointer widgets over other applications or games. This overlay operates passively and does not inspect or collect data from underlying windows.

4. Information Collection and Local Storage

Auto Touch does not require an account or sign-in. The app stores your custom profiles and tap/swipe coordinates locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this configuration. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process data as described in Section 5.

5. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Auto Touch uses the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display advertisements. Google's published Google Play disclosure for the SDK lists data that may be collected and shared automatically, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video ad views;
  • Diagnostics, such as app or SDK launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, and other signed-in account identifiers where applicable.

Google states these data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data handling can depend on the SDK version, app configuration, consent choices, and ad-serving mode. Data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The app currently integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK version 23.6.0; Google's disclosure page describes the latest SDK version, so we review the applicable disclosure when the SDK or configuration changes.

For details, see Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Children's Privacy

Auto Touch does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate corrective action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
