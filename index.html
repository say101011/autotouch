1. Overview

Welcome to Auto Touch. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Android application.

2. AccessibilityService API Usage Disclosure

In full compliance with the Google Play Accessibility API Policy, Auto Touch provides clear and prominent disclosure regarding its use of Android's AccessibilityService API:

• Purpose of Accessibility Service: Auto Touch uses the AccessibilityService API strictly to dispatch simulated tap and swipe gestures at specific screen coordinates configured by you.

• Zero Data Collection: The app does NOT read screen text, record keystrokes, access passwords, track locations, or read contacts. Absolutely no personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted via the Accessibility Service.

• Local Storage Only: All your custom configuration settings and swipe/tap target information are stored exclusively within your device's local storage (AutoClickStore) and are never sent to external servers.

• Third-Party Services: Auto Touch integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

3. Overlay Permission (Display Over Other Apps)

Auto Touch requests the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display Over Other Apps") permission solely to display the floating action controller and target pointer widgets over other applications or games. This overlay operates passively and does not inspect or collect data from underlying windows.

4. Information Collection

Auto Touch operates on a Zero Personal Data Collection principle:

  • We do NOT require user account creation or sign-in.
  • We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location data.

5. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Auto Touch integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable technical data (such as Advertising ID, device model, and general IP-based geographic location) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • For more information on Google AdMob's privacy practices, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

6. Children's Privacy

Auto Touch does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate corrective action.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
